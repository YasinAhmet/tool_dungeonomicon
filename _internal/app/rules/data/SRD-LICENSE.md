# The rules content of Dungeonomicon

The rules data in this folder (`*.json`) and its translations (`app/locale/content/*.csv`) are the System Reference
Document 5.1 only.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and
available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Changes: the SRD's content is stored in the program's own format (records of spells, creatures, items, classes...),
some of its wording is brought in line with the 2014 rules the program follows, and the list of which records are in
the SRD comes from the 5e-database project (https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database, MIT license).

Anything beyond the SRD - the rest of the Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual, the other
books, creature descriptions, tokens and art - is not part of the program. It can be added as content modules
(`.dmmod` files, **Settings ▸ Installations**), which carry their own terms.
