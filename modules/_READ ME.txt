Drop content modules (.dmmod files) into this folder.

Dungeonomicon reads them when it starts - and at once while it runs - and their books show up in the Sourcebooks
window. A file taken out of this folder takes its module with it.

This folder travels with the program: copy or zip the whole Dungeonomicon folder and give it to someone, and App.exe
starts on their computer with the same modules. (Settings, players and the bot token stay in your own
Documents\Dungeonomicon folder and are never part of it.)
