Drop content modules (.dmmod files) into this folder.

Dungeonomicon reads them when it starts - and at once while it runs - and their books show up in the Sourcebooks
window. A file taken out of this folder takes its module with it. Both apps of this folder read them: App.exe (the
campaign editor) and Battlegrounds.exe (duels, skirmishes and the arena).

This folder travels with the program: copy or zip the whole Dungeonomicon folder and give it to someone, and both
exes start on their computer with the same modules. (Characters, settings and the bot token stay in your own
Documents\Dungeonomicon and Documents\Dungeonomicon Battlegrounds folders and are never part of it.)
