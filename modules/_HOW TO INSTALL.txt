Dungeonomicon content modules (v1.1)

Put these .dmmod files into the "modules" folder next to App.exe (Dungeonomicon) or Battlegrounds.exe
(Dungeonomicon Battlegrounds) - unzipping this file into the program folder does it. The program reads them at start,
and at once while it runs; their books show up in the Sourcebooks window. Take only the ones you want.

  LGR-1.0.dmmod              Legends Reforged: Monsters, legendary weapons and spells from famous fantasy games (The Elder Scrolls, Enderal, The Witcher and more) and from world myth, rebuilt for 5e. Every creature has its own token, every spell and weapon its own icon.
  adventures-1.0.dmmod       Adventures: The races, items and creatures of the published adventures.
  book-art-1.0.dmmod         Creature and race art: The books' artwork of the creatures (on their cards and the battle map when a creature has no token) and a picture of each race (characters without a portrait).
  core-books-1.0.dmmod       Core rulebooks (beyond the SRD): Everything of the Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual that the SRD 5.1 leaves out - subclasses, races, backgrounds, feats, spells, magic items, creatures - with the books' own names for the SRD's renamed spells and items, and the creatures' descriptions.
  game-icons-1.0.dmmod       Action icons: The pictures of the action bar, the spells, the conditions over the tokens and the sound board's buttons.
  item-sprites-1.0.dmmod     Item sprites: Small pictures of the items lying on the battle maps and of the things thrown across them.
  map-props-1.0.dmmod        Map props: The pictures of the battle map's props and symbols: doors, furniture, traps, statues...
  monster-tokens-1.0.dmmod   Monster tokens: A token picture for nearly every creature of the books.
  portraits-bg-1.0.dmmod     Baldur's Gate portraits: Portraits to pick for characters and creatures in the portrait editor.
  settings-1.0.dmmod         Campaign settings: The campaign setting books: Eberron, Ravnica, Theros, Wildemount, Strixhaven, Spelljammer and more.
  supplements-1.0.dmmod      Supplements: The other rulebooks: Xanathar's Guide, Tasha's Cauldron, Volo's Guide, Mordenkainen's Tome, Fizban's Treasury and more, with Tasha's optional class features.
  table-sounds-1.0.dmmod     Table sounds: The audio manager's ambience loops and sound effects: rain, a campfire, a tavern, doors, swords, roars...
  world-art-1.0.dmmod        World map art: The world map's stamps: towns, castles, trees, mountains, ships - top-down, painted and ink sets.
